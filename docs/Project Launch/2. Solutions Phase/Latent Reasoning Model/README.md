# Singularis

Our Latent Reasoning Model, dubbed "Singularis", was born from the idea of leveraging existing reasoning model technology and generalizing them with the power of LLMs.

--- 
The concept of Singularis was to embed a reasoning model within the Encoder and Decoder layers of an encoder-decoder based LLM.

Our reasoning model of choice was called Universal Reasoning Model ([URM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.14693)), for it's ability to perform well on former ARC-AGI challenges and surpass Tiny Recursive Machine (TRM) and Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM).

Our LLM of choice was T5Gemma 2, for it's affordable size ranges (270M to 4B) and its performance in comparison to it's predecesors T5Gemma and Flan-T5
