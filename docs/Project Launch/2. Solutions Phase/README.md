# Solutions Phase

During the Solutions Phase, our team of 6 split into 3 teams of two based on similar studied topics during the Research Phase. The purpose of these sub-teams was to produce "solutions" or ideas that could be potentially developed into a working solution for our concept of a reasoning model.

Here we developed 2 core solutions:

1. [The Latent Reasoning Model](Latent%20Reasoning%20Model/README.md):
    - A reasoning model that leverages the general intelligence progress that LLMs have made thus far with the power of recursion induced reasoning

2. [The World Model](World%20Model/README.md):
    - An Intelligent System that is capable of solving ARC-AGI-3 problems

We decided to move forward to the Development Phase with both solutions rather than pruning one, because we thought to cover all basis. Our latent reasoning model is a generalized reasoning system and our world model attempts ARC-AGI-3.
